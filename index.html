Subscription

  • PDFena is used with a monthly subscription. The first 7 days are free; if the subscription is not cancelled before the trial ends, it renews at the price shown in the App Store.
  • One subscription covers PDFena on Mac, iPad and iPhone with the same Apple Account.
  • Cancel any time in the App Store: your account ▸ Subscriptions. The subscription then stays active until the end of the period already paid.
  • Bought on another device, or after reinstalling: Restore Purchases on the Subscription screen.

Common questions

Where are my documents? Where you keep them. PDFena opens and saves files in place, in any folder, iCloud Drive or a file provider. It has no cloud of its own.

Signing on the iPad for the Mac. Both devices need Local Network allowed for PDFena (System Settings ▸ Privacy & Security ▸ Local Network on the Mac, Settings ▸ Privacy & Security ▸ Local Network on the iPad) and must be on the same Wi-Fi or within Bluetooth range.
